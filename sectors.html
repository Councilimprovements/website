Sectors

We understand how public organisations work.

Different sites bring different pressures. Here is how we help in each sector.

Councils and town centres

Typical challenge

Tired walls, underpasses and vacant sites that attract tagging and fly-posting. Limited budgets, and a need to show residents and members that money is well spent.

How we help

We run proper consultation, provide clear documentation for procurement and approvals, and deliver murals that give people a reason to feel proud of their town centre.

Talk to us about your site: Councils and town centres

Schools and colleges

Typical challenge

Bare playground walls and boundary fences. Any work must fit around the school day and meet safeguarding requirements.

How we help

Pupils help to shape the design through workshops. We paint during holidays or outside school hours where possible, and follow your safeguarding procedures on site.

Talk to us about your site: Schools and colleges

Hospitals and NHS sites

Typical challenge

Stark corridors, courtyards and entrances that can feel clinical or unwelcoming for patients, visitors and staff.

How we help

We create calm, nature-led designs agreed with staff and patient groups. We plan our work around your site rules, access restrictions and infection-control requirements.

Talk to us about your site: Hospitals and NHS sites

BIDs and business owners

Typical challenge

Empty units, closed shutters and blank side walls that make a high street feel quiet.

How we help

We turn shutters and walls into a reason to visit. We work around trading hours, keep access clear and speak to neighbouring businesses before we start.

Talk to us about your site: BIDs and business owners

Housing providers

Typical challenge

Gable ends, stairwells, bin stores and shared spaces that are repeatedly damaged, and residents who feel their estate is overlooked.

How we help

We involve residents in the design, so the finished mural belongs to them. We keep disruption low and agree working hours with you in advance.

Talk to us about your site: Housing providers

Tell us about your site and what you would like it to achieve. We will come back to you with clear next steps and no obligation.
