About us

Public art with a public purpose.

We exist to enhance and improve public spaces, and to reduce anti-social behaviour and vandalism, by turning blank or neglected walls into murals that communities are proud of.

Our mission

Better places, made with the people who use them.

A blank or damaged wall says that nobody is looking after a place. A well-made mural says the opposite. It shows that a space is valued, and it gives people something to enjoy and talk about.

Council Improvements works with public bodies and businesses to make that change happen safely, on time and with local support.

Our values

Four things we will not compromise on.

Quality

Careful design, proper surface preparation and durable exterior paints. We want the work to look good for years.

Safety

Every job is planned, risk-assessed and supervised. We protect the public, your staff and our team.

Community

Local people are involved from the start. The best murals belong to the place they are in.

Reliability

Clear quotes, agreed dates and one point of contact. We do what we say we will do.

Artists and local people

The right artist for the right wall.

We match each project with an artist whose style suits the site and the brief. Where we can, we work with artists who know the area.

Before any design is drawn, we listen. That can mean a survey, a drop-in session, a school workshop or a conversation with neighbouring businesses. What we hear shapes the design.

You see and approve the concept before any paint goes on the wall.

Have a wall in mind?

Tell us about your site and what you would like it to achieve. We will come back to you with clear next steps and no obligation.
