Services

Everything needed to deliver a mural, from first idea to long-term care.

Four services that work on their own or together. Each one is managed by a single point of contact.

Public murals

We design and paint large-scale murals for walls, gable ends, underpasses, shutters, hoardings and courtyards.

  • Site survey and wall condition check
  • Concept designs for you to review and approve
  • Surface preparation, priming and painting with exterior-grade paints
  • Access equipment, permits and public safety managed for you
  • Reused paint sourced where suitable, as a more sustainable option

Anti-graffiti and vandalism deterrence

For walls that are tagged again and again, a mural can be a practical, lasting alternative to repeated cleaning.

  • Assessment of the wall and the pattern of damage
  • A design that covers the whole surface, leaving no blank space
  • Optional anti-graffiti coating so marks can be cleaned off more easily
  • A simple plan for reporting and removing any new tagging quickly

Community and school co-design

People look after what they helped to create. We run sessions that let residents, pupils, patients or traders shape the design.

  • Workshops for schools, youth groups and community groups
  • Surveys and drop-in sessions for the wider public
  • Designs that reflect local history, nature and identity
  • A short consultation summary you can share with stakeholders

Aftercare and maintenance

Our work does not stop when the paint dries. We help you keep the mural in good condition.

  • A handover pack with paint colours, coating details and care advice
  • Scheduled inspections
  • Cleaning and touch-ups if the wall is marked or damaged
  • Refresh and repaint options as the mural ages

Plain explanation

How does a mural deter tagging?

A cared-for wall is less likely to be defaced

Blank, neglected walls invite tagging. A finished mural shows that someone looks after the space. Many taggers also choose not to paint over artwork, and a busy design leaves little clear space for a tag to stand out.

A protective coating makes cleaning easier

Murals can be finished with a clear anti-graffiti coating. If the wall is marked, the mark can usually be cleaned off without damaging the artwork underneath.

Quick repairs keep it that way

Removing any damage promptly helps to stop it building up. Our aftercare service is there to help with this.

No mural can guarantee that a wall will never be tagged. We will give you an honest view of what is likely to work on your site.

Have a wall in mind?

Tell us about your site and what you would like it to achieve. We will come back to you with clear next steps and no obligation.
