How we work

A clear process, from first conversation to aftercare.

Six steps. You know what happens at each one, who is responsible and what you need to approve.

Our six steps

  1. Initial conversation and site visit

    We talk through your aims, budget and timescale. Then we visit the site to check the wall, access and surroundings.

  2. Consultation and concept design

    We gather views from the people who use the space. Our artist turns these into concept designs for you to review.

  3. Approvals, permissions and safety planning

    We help with landowner consent and any planning questions. We prepare risk assessments, method statements and a programme of work.

  4. Painting

    We prepare the surface and paint the mural. The site is kept safe and tidy, and you get regular updates.

  5. Handover

    We walk the finished wall with you, apply any protective coating and give you a handover pack with care advice.

  6. Aftercare

    We stay in touch. Inspections, cleaning and touch-ups are available to keep the mural in good condition.

Assurance

Working with public bodies

We know you need evidence, not promises. The documents and assurances we can provide are listed here.

  • Public liability insurance
  • Risk assessments and method statements (RAMS) for every project
  • Enhanced DBS checks where required
  • Safeguarding policy
  • Health and safety accreditation
  • Planning and landowner consent support
  • A named project manager for every project
