How we collect, use and look after your personal information.

Who we are

Council Improvements (company number ), based in , is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

You can contact us about this policy using our enquiry form.

What information we collect

When you use our enquiry form, we collect the details you give us. These may include:

  • your name, organisation and role
  • your email address and phone number
  • details of your site and project, and anything else you include in your message

Why we use it and our lawful basis

We use your information to reply to your enquiry, prepare proposals and, if you become a client, deliver and manage your project.

Under UK GDPR, our lawful bases are:

  • Legitimate interests: replying to enquiries and running our business.
  • Contract: taking steps before entering into a contract with you, and carrying it out.
  • Legal obligation: keeping records we are required to keep, such as accounts.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you have asked us to.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with suppliers who help us run our business, and only as far as they need it:

  • Formspree, which processes submissions from our enquiry form and sends them to us. Formspree is based in the United States.
  • The providers of our email, IT and business software.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry. If you become a client, we keep project and financial records for as long as the law requires.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or tracking tools. Our web hosting provider may record technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes.

Your rights

Under data protection law, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of your information
  • ask us to correct information you think is wrong
  • ask us to delete your information, in certain circumstances
  • ask us to limit how we use your information, or object to our use of it
  • ask us to transfer your information to you or to another organisation, in certain circumstances

To use any of these rights, contact us using our enquiry form. We will respond within one month.

How to complain

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator. Visit ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint (opens in a new tab).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
