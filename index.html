Council Improvements  |  Murals and public art for public spaces

We turn blank and neglected walls into public art that communities are proud of.

Our mission is to enhance and improve public spaces, and to reduce anti-social behaviour and vandalism, in town and city centres across the UK.

Who we are

A professional partner for public art.

Council Improvements designs and paints murals for councils, schools, hospitals, business improvement districts, housing providers and businesses.

We know that commissioning public art can feel like a risk. So we make it simple. We consult local people, handle the planning and safety paperwork, paint with care, and look after the wall afterwards.

What we aim for

Every project works towards three aims.

Safer-feeling streets

We aim to make neglected corners feel cared for, so people feel more comfortable using them.

Proud communities

We aim to create artwork that local people helped to shape and want to look after.

Stronger town-centre footfall

We aim to give people a reason to visit, stop and spend time in the places you manage.

These are our aims for every project. Results vary from place to place, and we do not promise specific outcomes.
